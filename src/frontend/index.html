Quantitative research for financial markets, sectors, and individual companies. Our mission is to empower investors and traders with data-driven insights and tools to support long-term wealth creation.

What we do

MyQuantDesk builds research frameworks and analytical tools across asset classes and market segments. We combine quantitative models, fundamental analysis, and market microstructure to:

  • Analyze markets, sectors, and companies using systematic approaches.
  • Develop tools for risk assessment, scenario analysis, and strategy evaluation.
  • Provide educational content that bridges quantitative concepts and practical trading/investing.

Our current live tools include analytics for Indian derivatives (NIFTY options), with more research modules and dashboards under development.
