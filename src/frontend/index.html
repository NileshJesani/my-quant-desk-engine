What we do

MyQuantDesk is a research-focused platform that provides fundamental and technical research on financial markets, sectors, and individual companies. We build quantitative models and real-time tools that help traders and investors measure risk and make more informed decisions.

Our tools work on live market data and are designed for:

  • Options traders who need Greeks and risk metrics in real time.
  • Investors looking for data-driven views on sectors and stocks.
  • Quant enthusiasts building and testing systematic strategies.
