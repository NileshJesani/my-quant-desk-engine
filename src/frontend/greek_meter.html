Greek Risk Meter

Overview

This tool will show your portfolio Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega) and how they change with moves in NIFTY, IV, and time. Currently a placeholder – full functionality coming soon.

What you’ll see here

  • Net delta, gamma, theta, and vega for your positions.
  • Stress tests: impact of ±1% move in NIFTY, ±5–10% move in IV.
  • Time decay projection over days/weeks.
